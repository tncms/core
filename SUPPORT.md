# Support

Where to go depends on what you need.

## Core bugs

Open a GitHub issue on this repository with your TNCMS version, environment
(PHP/DB/host), clear steps to reproduce, and what you expected versus what
happened.

## Feature requests

Open a GitHub issue describing the problem you want solved and your proposed
behavior. Keep requests focused on **Core**; extension ideas belong with the
relevant extension.

## Security reports

Do **not** use public issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Community questions / how-to

General "how do I…" questions are best asked through the community channels
linked at <https://tncms.org>, not the issue tracker.

## Separately distributed extensions

Page Builder, Ecommerce, Knowledge Library, Monetization, and other optional or
commercial extensions are **not** part of Core and are supported through their
own channels. Core's MIT license does not include support for those products.

---

This is a volunteer-driven beta project. We do not offer a guaranteed response
time or commercial support commitment.
